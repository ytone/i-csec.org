Message from Saga Branch Head

Mr. Hironori Miura

For more than 30 years, I have worked to improve computer science and digital education in Saga Prefecture, serving as both a teacher and an educational administrator.

Saga was once recognized as one of Japan's leading regions in ICT education. However, as we enter the age of AI, I believe there is still significant potential for further growth and innovation.

In April 2026, I was honored to be appointed as the Saga Branch Representative of ICSEC (International Computer Science Education Consortium).

Through this role, I hope to contribute to ICSEC's international community while also helping connect educators and stakeholders in Saga with colleagues and initiatives around the world. By building these connections, I aim to support the continued advancement of AI and computer science education in the region.

I look forward to working with partners both in Japan and internationally to create more opportunities for students to thrive in the digital era.
