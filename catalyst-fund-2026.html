How Applications Are Assessed

Selection Criteria

Applications will be reviewed based on the following three criteria.

01

Contextually Innovative

Addresses genuine local educational needs through approaches that are rooted in the local context and may inspire others.

02

Execution Capability

Demonstrates the experience, commitment, and capacity required to successfully implement the proposed project.

03

Learning Value for the ICSEC Community

Generates insights, practices, or resources that can be shared and adapted across the ICSEC community.
